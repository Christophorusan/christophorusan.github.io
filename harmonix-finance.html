Oslo Analytics

Oslo is a growth workspace I built for protocol teams. It brings product use, channel performance, and wallet activity—deposits, withdrawals, and retention—into one view. The contact list shows who a person is, how to reach them, and, when their wallet is identified, what they hold. Teams can spot where users drop off, find prospects, and turn the data into outreach and product improvements.
