Oslo Analytics

I built Oslo as Harmonix’s analytics layer, bringing X and community activity together with Hyperliquid wallet and vault flows. It maps the journey from campaign reach and UTM activity to deposits, then surfaces monthly new and active wallets, deposit and withdrawal trends, and retention signals. The calendar connects those insights to content, experiments, and follow-up actions.
