01

Social Media Growth Strategy

Target crypto-native users, first-timers, institutions, and retail — with a full-funnel strategy whose goal is buying retention. Campaigns and content are designed to support each stage of the funnel and build long-term loyalty to the protocol's products: the website and mobile app, the core products (vaults, strategies, yield-bearing tokens), and the native token.

Content runs in three formats at every stage:

  • Videos — ads, explainers, tutorials, interviews
  • Written — website copy, blogs, newsletters, protocol docs
  • Memes & graphics — visuals that capture attention and reinforce the message
Funnel · Stage 1

Outreach

The first thing people see should be short and carry one core function of the product as the message — catch attention and pull users to the next part of the funnel, the website.

Measure: click-through rate against impressions, then engagement and shares — and above all, landing-page visits.

Video. Short ad videos (~30s) that hook users as the first touch.

e.g.HYPE TGE adAave

Written. The first pitch: what the protocol is, its core features, and why it matters — assuming zero prior knowledge. Explain the product, highlight features in plain terms, guide onboarding step by step, build trust with consistent messaging, and close with a clear call to action.

e.g.Phantom Perps

Memes & designs. Graphics that ride world news, protocol campaigns, roadmaps, and product updates — and pitch the product to first-time users.

e.g.BaseAave

Funnel · Stage 2

Conversion

Turn visitors into active participants.

Measure: first-time deposits relative to site visits — new users and first-time depositors, tracked from impression to deposit.

Video. Onboarding videos: create a wallet, fund it, deposit into the protocol, security and risk (hack prevention, key safety), and protocol architecture. Package them as an "Academy hub" — a 101 with levels, from what stablecoins and vaults are, to where the yield comes from, to full technical architecture.

e.g.Shorts exampleExplainer example

Written. A written onboarding path, plus blogs and newsletters explaining product functions, how they work, and how to use them. Split the blog by audience: 101 for non-crypto readers, newsletter & updates, and how-tos (deposit, borrow, stake, trade, swap).

e.g.Hyperliquid onboarding docs

Memes & designs. One core message — download, deposit, earn, trade — explained in extremely simple terms: what it is, how it works, the value proposition.

e.g.BaseMorpho

Funnel · Stage 3

Retention

The stage the whole strategy is built to serve: users who deposit, stay, engage with new products — and go on to hold the token.

Measure: monthly active users and cohort activity — how many depositors keep using the protocol, and how many go on to buy the token and engage with new releases.

Video. Conference presentations, annual updates, CEO interviews — the vision of the protocol, what the team is working on, and why.

e.g.PresentationInterview

Written. What's next, in simple terms: roadmaps, product vision, and how the protocol scales — so users understand where it's heading.

e.g.EthenaPendle

Memes & designs. Build community, not just brand recognition — memes as long-term alignment with partners and supporters, like wearing the t-shirt of your favorite club.

e.g.HyperliquidSolana

Brand

Focus the brand on solving a specific problem in a target ecosystem. Start with a niche, then expand — Ethereum first, then Solana or Hyperliquid. Goals: grow FDV and market value, increase token holders and community engagement, expand partners and builders, build power users who drive adoption, and attract and retain developers.

e.g.Morpho brandMorpho launch

Narratives

Use data-driven messages that perform organically or ride trending topics, then amplify across every channel: community, KOLs, newsletters, partners, events, team pages, and ads. Narrative pillars: valuation & growth, protocol health (TVL, liquidity, audits, retention, solvency), community & partners, the token as community-first ownership, technical depth, and exchange features.

02

Product & Growth

A framework for collaboration between product and growth teams, supported by data and performance tracking.

Team 1 — Product

Build, secure, and maintain the protocol
  • Engineering: contracts, vaults, integrations, front-end
  • Ops / validators: monitor vaults and performance
  • Security: audits, bounties, multisig, user protection
  • Design & UX: seamless vault experiences
  • User support: onboarding and troubleshooting
  • Analytics: TVL, usage, protocol performance

Team 2 — Growth

Drive adoption, engagement, ecosystem growth
  • Social & brand: campaigns, content, product updates
  • Incentives & programs: rewards for power users and builders
  • Partnerships & community: KOLs, private groups
  • Market research: user behavior, whales, trends
  • Education & feedback: tutorials, retention support

Roadmap

One annual roadmap aligning product releases with growth execution: product defines launch and update timelines; growth builds distribution around each release. The roadmap tracks monthly performance metrics and both teams' execution dates.

Distribution

Distribution follows a four-stage loop:

01Design
02Test
03Execute
04Review
  • Design — define target users, regions, and partners from on-chain and social data (DeBank, Etherscan, X, geographic hubs).
  • Test — one core message, 3–5 variations, tested organically across accounts and formats; pick winners on CTR, engagement, and conversion.
  • Execute — deploy winning assets across social, newsletters, email, KOLs, partners, media, events, community, and education.
  • Review — measure clicks, users, deposits, retention; double down on what works; repeat.

External agencies

An annual budget for partners and agencies that build trust, reputation, and retention — targeted by ecosystem, location, and language. If the target is Tokyo, that means an article, podcast, or blog in a trusted Japanese outlet, so a search for the protocol surfaces a clear explanation. Every campaign is measured and reviewed for profitability and its support of the wider funnel.

e.g.BanklessOnChain Times on Aerodrome

Partners

A repeatable co-marketing process: set objectives and results with each partner, design materials that support the funnel and community initiatives, then use data to gather feedback and improve the process each cycle.

e.g.GauntletFelix

Social campaigns & community incentives

This is who it's all for: the community of partners, users, builders, and investors. Campaigns target five groups — power users (feedback, content campaigns, NFTs), DAO contributors (governance, foundation, grants, treasury), builders (hackathons, security contests), events (investors, builders, partners, community), and mindshare (ambassador programs, KOLs, narrative control).

Community incentives: points programs, giveaways, competitions, leaderboards — points on yield and curated services for power users.

e.g.FelixNikita BierMaple

03

Data & Performance Tracking

The protocol generates value through token appreciation and product fees. Performance tracking grows both systematically, in a recurring four-stage loop:

01Set objectives
02Measure
03Forecast
04Improve
  • Set objectives — one primary long-term objective (users, TVL, FDV, token holders), broken into annual and monthly milestones on a shared roadmap and calendar.
  • Measure — funnel conversion rates at each stage; current position in users, TVL, FDV, and holders; how efficiently growth translates into revenue. Retention is read through inflows versus outflows — net retention above 100% means users increase exposure over time rather than churn.
  • Forecast — model the steady-state growth rate needed to hit each milestone, and check monthly performance against it.
  • Improve — allocate budget to the highest-ROI activities: retention initiatives, funnel optimization, distribution scaling. Learnings feed directly into the next round of tests and campaigns.
