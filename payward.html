Payward

Advertisement concept · Copywriting & video editing · 2026

A 35-second advertisement concept exploring the evolution from cash to digital money and 24/7 finance. Developed in the context of Kraken’s US perpetual futures launch, it introduces Payward to developers, users, and investors worldwide.

The idea

Tell the story of modern finance through something everyone understands: money changing hands. Cash, gold, and trading floors give way to digital payments and a connected world, placing Payward’s products at the heart of the next evolution of finance.

The aim is to help developers, users, and investors understand Payward’s role in how people build financial products, move money, and access markets. The message connects these uses to values familiar to crypto-native audiences: financial freedom, open access, and control over money.

My contribution

I wrote the copy and edited reference footage into a concept advertisement, establishing the story, visual direction, and pacing without a production budget.

This is a starting point for production. A finished advertisement could combine original filmed footage and AI-generated imagery to create a distinctive visual identity for Payward.
