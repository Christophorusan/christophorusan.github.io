Oslo

A self-custody wallet that brings payments, prediction markets, perps, and earning into one clear place.

One wallet. Four ways to put it to work.

I wanted a crypto wallet that made lending, perpetuals, swaps, bridges, and prediction markets feel like parts of one product. The visual language stays calm and Scandinavian, with Swiss-style typography and deliberate color cues to make each choice easier to scan.

01 / Wallet

Start with a clear view of the whole portfolio, then make the next action easy to find.

The wallet is the home for everything else. I brought balances, holdings, and everyday actions into one view, then connected that starting point to the broader set of open-finance tools. The portfolio shown is illustrative sample data; no wallet is connected.

Open the Oslo landing page ↗

02 / Perps

Keep the essentials visible together, so a market decision needs less hunting around.

A live chart, order book, and trade controls share one focused workspace. The quieter neutral surfaces keep attention on price movement and the buy or sell decision; account values stay hidden while disconnected.

Open Perps in Oslo ↗

03 / Prediction markets

Make each outcome and its probability easy to compare at a glance.

Categories, event cards, probability, and volume create a quick reading path from discovery to comparison. I aligned the YES and NO cues with the same green and red used for long and short in Perps, so those signals stay consistent across the product.

Open Prediction Markets in Oslo ↗

04 / Lending

Put rates, collateral, and market size in the same reading frame.

Supply and borrow rates, collateral eligibility, and market totals help people compare before committing. Lending sits alongside the wallet and trading tools, while its provider and network stay visible.
