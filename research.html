Oslo

A self-custody wallet for open finance: send, receive, borrow, trade, and discover protocols across Hyperliquid, HyperEVM, and other networks.

One wallet. Four ways to put it to work.

I built Oslo on Hyperliquid and designed it to connect Hyperliquid markets with HyperEVM and other networks. The product centers on self-custody: people keep control of their wallets and keys while getting a simple way to send, receive, borrow against eligible assets, trade, and discover protocols. The goal is to make access to open finance feel as straightforward as logging onto the internet. I used Scandinavian product simplicity, Swiss typography, and neuromarketing principles as a lens for a clear color hierarchy that makes actions and outcomes easier to scan.

Perps

Keep the essentials visible together, so a market decision needs less hunting around.

Open Perps ↗

A live chart, order book, and trade controls share one focused workspace. The quieter neutral surfaces keep attention on price movement and the buy or sell decision; account values stay hidden while disconnected.

Prediction markets

Make each outcome and its probability easy to compare at a glance.

Open Predictions ↗

Categories, event cards, probability, and volume create a quick reading path from discovery to comparison. I aligned the YES and NO cues with the same green and red used for long and short in Perps, so those signals stay consistent across the product.
