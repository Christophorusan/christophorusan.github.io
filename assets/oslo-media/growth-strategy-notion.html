1. Social Media Growth Strategy

Targeting crypto-native and first-time users, institutions, and retail. We follow a full-funnel strategy with the goal of buying retention — focusing on acquiring long-term valuable users rather than short-term point farmers.
Content runs in three formats:
Videos: ads, explainers, tutorials, interviews
Written: website copy, blogs, newsletters, and protocol documentation
Memes & graphics: engaging visuals that capture attention and reinforce messaging
These campaigns pay for retention of the protocol's core products: the website & mobile app, the core products (vaults, strategies, yield-bearing tokens), and the native protocol token.

The Funnel

Design campaigns and content to support each stage of the funnel, with the objective of paying to retain users and building long-term loyalty.
Outreach — track click-through rate (CTR) to see how many people engage with your content
Conversion — measure new users and first-time depositors to understand how many visitors become active participants
Retention — track monthly active users (MAU) and cohort activity to see how many users keep using the protocol

Outreach

The first thing people see should be short and convey one core function of the product as the message — catch attention and pull users to the next part of the funnel, the website.
Measure: CTR vs. impressions, then engagement and shares — and most importantly, landing-page visits.
Videos — short ad videos (~30s) that hook users as the first part of the funnel.
Written — the first pitch: what the protocol is, its core features, and why it matters, assuming no prior knowledge. Explain the protocol, highlight key features in simple terms, guide onboarding step by step, build trust with clear consistent messaging, and end with a clear call to action.

Brand

Focus the brand on solving a specific problem in a target ecosystem (e.g. Ethereum). Start with a niche, then expand to other ecosystems like Solana or Hyperliquid.
Goals:
Grow FDV and market value
Increase token holders and community engagement
Expand partners and builders
Build power users who drive adoption
Attract and retain developers

Narratives

Use data-driven messages that perform well organically or align with trending topics. Amplify them across all channels: community, KOLs, newsletters, partners, events, team pages, and ads.
Narrative pillars: valuation & growth · protocol health (TVL, liquidity, audits, stress tests, retention, solvency, risk) · community & partners · a community-first, user-owned token · technical depth (spot, perps, mobile app, ecosystem partners) · exchange features.

2. Product & Growth

A framework for collaboration between product and growth teams to achieve protocol objectives efficiently, supported by data and performance tracking.

Team 1 — Product

Focus: build, secure, and maintain the protocol
Engineering: develop and maintain smart contracts, vaults, integrations, and front-end
Ops / Validators: monitor vaults, transactions, and overall protocol performance
Security: audits, bug bounties, multisig, 2FA, user protection
Design & UX: user-friendly interfaces and seamless vault experiences
User support: onboarding, troubleshooting, general assistance
Analytics: insights on TVL, usage, and protocol performance

Team 2 — Growth

Focus: drive adoption, engagement, and ecosystem growth
Social & brand: campaigns, content, and product updates to grow awareness
Incentives & programs: rewards and trading programs for power users and builders
Partnerships & community: KOLs, private groups, and communities
Market research & analytics: user behavior, whales, and market trends
Education & feedback: tutorials, user feedback, retention support

Roadmap

Maintain a single annual roadmap aligning product releases with growth execution:
Product defines launch and update timelines
Growth builds and executes distribution strategies around each release
The roadmap tracks monthly performance metrics, growth execution dates, and product execution dates.

Distribution Strategy

Distribution follows a four-stage loop: design, test, execute, review.
Design — define target users, regions, and partners using on-chain and social data (DeBank, Etherscan, X, geographic hubs)
Test — create one core message, produce 3–5 variations, test organically across accounts and formats, select winners on CTR, engagement, and conversion
Execute — deploy winning assets across social, newsletters, email; KOLs, partners, media, events; community and education initiatives
Review — measure impact (clicks, users, deposits, retention), double down on what works, repeat
